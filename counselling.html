Counselling Services

Students in their early academic stage need the help of an expert who acts as their mentor, guide, and well-wisher. At KLU CS&IT, we initiate counseling of all sorts at different stages, or students can approach us directly when in need.

🧠

Psychological Counselling

Professionally trained psychologists counsel students on personal and interpersonal issues, addressing social, vocational, emotional, academic, and health concerns.

📚

Academic Counselling

Academic counselors assist in selecting the right course per career growth perspective, assessing mental abilities and academic interests to suggest appropriate programs.

💼

Career Counselling

Counselors help students identify the right job that suits their personality traits, guiding them toward a thriving and prospering career.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parent Counselling

Regular parent-teacher interactions and counselling sessions to keep families informed about student progress and career opportunities.
