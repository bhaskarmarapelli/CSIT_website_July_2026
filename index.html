About CS&IT

Where Engineering Fundamentals
Meet Real-World Practice

The B.Tech in Computer Science & Information Technology, established in 2020 at KL University Vijayawada, delivers an industry-integrated curriculum shaped by partner companies like Wipro, Google, Cisco and Salesforce.

Students graduate with strong hands-on skills in software development, cloud architecture, AI/ML, DevOps, and cybersecurity — backed by 60% practical learning and recognised global certifications.
