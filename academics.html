Industry-Integrated Curriculum · 20:1 Ratio

Academic Overview

KL University has an outstanding reputation for student success, teaching, research and innovation. Our School of Computer Science and Engineering features an Industry Integrated Curriculum with a 20:1 student-to-faculty ratio. The Teaching-Learning Process focuses on 60% Hands-on (Practical) and 40% Theory. Students must acquire 160+ credits as part of their degree requirements.

Credit Distribution: Core courses + Professional Electives + Open Electives + MOOCs + Capstone Project + Internship = 160+ Credits
Academic Calender: Academic Calender

Year-wise Academic Structure

1⃣

First Year — Core Sciences

Emphasis on Basic Sciences and Engineering Sciences. Logic building through coding challenges on HackerRank, HackerEarth, and CodeChef.

2⃣

Second Year — Core Courses

Deep dive into Computer Science fundamentals. Courses designed per Bloom's Taxonomy levels with lecture, tutorial, practical, and skilling components.

3⃣

Third Year — Specialization

Industry-readiness programs through Wipro (TalentNext), Salesforce, ServiceNow, Pega, EPAM, UI Path, CISCO, and Google.

4⃣

Final Year — Projects & Internship

Open Electives, capstone projects, and full-year internships at reputed MNCs. 50+ students annually enrolled in Practice School.

Innovative Learning Methods

  • Peer Learning, Flipped Learning, Project Based Learning
  • Hackathons as course evaluation tools
  • Lab Taken to Class (LTC) and Project Based Labs
  • 10% evaluation via Coursera, SWAYAM/NPTEL
