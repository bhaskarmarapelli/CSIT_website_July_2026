About B.Tech CS&IT

The B.Tech in Computer Science & Information Technology (CS&IT), established in 2020, offers an industry-focused curriculum supported by highly experienced faculty. The department nurtures students through a balanced approach of conceptual learning, hands-on practice, and continuous mentorship, ensuring excellence in both academics and professional development.

This four-year program blends engineering fundamentals with modern, tool-driven learning. Students gain strong technical expertise in software development, system design, cloud environments, and emerging technologies. The curriculum emphasizes prototype building, real-time project implementation, and industry-ready skill development, making graduates immediately employable and future-ready.

With global demand for IT professionals rapidly increasing, this program equips students with a powerful combination of theoretical foundation and practical exposure. The curriculum evolves regularly to include cutting-edge technologies, preparing students to excel in careers such as data engineering, DevOps, AI engineering, cloud architecture, cybersecurity, and more.
