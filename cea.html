Centre for Extension Activities

The CSIT department constituted the Centre for Extension Activities to imbibe social consciousness into the student community, organizing philanthropic activities and providing engineering solutions for social problems.

👧 Children & Youth Wing

Eradicating child labor, creating awareness in villages about the importance of education, and guiding youth toward productive futures.

🏘 Rural Development Wing

Health check-up campaigns, awareness of contagious diseases, first aid classes, and rural infrastructure development initiatives.

📢 Social Awareness Wing

RTI/RTE awareness programs, empowering people with knowledge about their rights, and combating corruption through information.

🎓 Student Sensitization Wing

Increasing social consciousness among students by involving them in diverse social service activities and community engagement.
