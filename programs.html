Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

💼

PEO 1 — Industry Ready

Practice engineering in a broad range of industrial, societal and real-world applications.

📚

PEO 2 — Lifelong Learners

Pursue advanced education, research, and development, and other creative and innovative efforts in science, engineering, and technology, as well as other professional careers.

🌐

PEO 3 — Ethical Leaders

Conduct themselves in a responsible, professional, and ethical manner.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

  • PSO 1 — An ability to Identify, Design, and Analyze complex computer systems, Implement and Interpret the results from those systems.
  • PSO 2 — An ability to select and apply current techniques, skills, and tools necessary for computing practice and integrate IT-based solutions into the user environment effectively.
